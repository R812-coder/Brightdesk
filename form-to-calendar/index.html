Questions

Which time zone are events booked in?

The chosen calendar's own time zone, never your computer's or the respondent's. A 10:00 answer on a calendar set to Australia/Perth is 10:00 in Perth, and everyone else's Calendar shows it in their own time.

Do I need a spreadsheet?

No. Answers are read straight from the form response.

Who does it run as?

The account that turned it on. Events are created with that account's calendar access, even when the form is closed. The dashboard shows "Running as" so everyone on the form knows.

What happens if an event can't be added?

Every event is re-read after it is created. Problems from Google (limits, a temporary error, lost access) are retried automatically, and you get at most one email a day saying what failed and how to fix it.

Are there limits?

Not from us on events. Google limits how many emails and invitations an account can send each day (about 100 email recipients a day on personal Gmail, 1,500 on Google Workspace).

What data does it access?

The current form's questions and responses, and events on the calendars you choose. Nothing is sent to BrightDesk. Read the privacy policy.
