Billing, invoices and cancelling

Update your card, download invoices, switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel. Sign in with the email you paid with; Stripe emails you a one-time code.

Open billing portal

Check your plan in the add-on

Open your form, then Extensions, Form to Calendar Bookings, Open. The bottom of the dashboard shows your plan and the date it runs until, for example "Pro until 29 Oct 2027". Choose Licence to see or remove the key.

Find or activate your licence key

  1. Search your inbox for "Form to Calendar Bookings licence key". It comes from BrightDesk.
  2. In the add-on choose Upgrade, paste it under Licence key, then Enter licence key.
  3. Can't find it? Ask us to resend it, using the email you paid with.

Renewals and when a key ends

Every key carries an end date matching what you've paid for. When you renew, we email a new key with the new end date; paste it the same way.

If a key ends, nothing is deleted. Pro options stop applying, your settings are kept, and your first form keeps adding events on the free plan. Paste a new key and Pro is back straight away.

Use it on a different Google account or domain

Pro keys work for one Google account email; Organization keys work for one Google Workspace domain. To move yours, tell us the old and new address or domain. We reissue keys at no cost.

Organization plan: give it to your team

Share the one key with your staff. Anyone signed in with an account on your domain can paste it in their own sidebar. A Google Workspace admin can install the add-on for everyone from the Admin console (Apps, Google Workspace Marketplace apps).

Cancelling and refunds

Cancel any time in the billing portal. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then the add-on returns to the free plan. If it didn't work for you, contact us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. Full terms are on the terms page.

Anything else: brightdesk.dev/support. We usually reply within 24 hours.
