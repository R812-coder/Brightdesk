Form to Calendar Bookings

Each Google Forms™ response becomes a Google Calendar™ event, at the right time, in the right calendar.

Set up in three steps

  1. Pick a calendar. Only calendars you can add events to are listed, each with its time zone.
  2. Match your questions. Choose the date question, the start time (or all-day) and how the event ends: a fixed length, a duration question, an end time or an end date.
  3. Turn it on. Test with the latest response first: it shows the exact event and creates nothing.

Time zones

Times are booked in the chosen calendar's own time zone, not your computer's and not the respondent's. A response of 10:00 on a calendar set to Australia/Perth is 10:00 in Perth for everyone who views it. Change a calendar's zone in Google Calendar, Settings, then open Edit setup and Save changes.

If someone picks a time that doesn't exist because the clocks move forward that night, the response is not booked and you are told. Nothing is moved silently.

All-day and multi-day events

  • No start time question, or a blank start time: an all-day event.
  • Ends: End date question: an all-day event from the start date to the end date, both included.

Date questions need the year

A date question that hides the year can't say which year to book. Setup shows Fix it, which turns on Include year for you.

Invitations

Turn on Invite the person who responded to add them as a guest. Google Calendar sends the invitation. To use the address they sign in with, turn on email collection in the form's Settings, Responses. Google limits how many invitations and emails an account can send each day.

When something goes wrong

  • Every event is re-read after it is created. If it is missing or at the wrong time, it is logged as Failed.
  • Problems from Google (limits, a temporary error, lost access) are retried up to 24 times: on the next response, and each time the person who turned it on opens this sidebar. Retry now on the dashboard tries straight away.
  • You get at most one email a day listing what failed and how to fix it.
  • Extensions, Form to Calendar Bookings, Activity log shows the last 200 actions.

Who it runs as

It runs from the account that turned it on ("Running as ..." on the dashboard), even when the form is closed. Events are created with that account's calendar access. If that person loses access to the form or leaves your organization, events stop. Another editor can turn it on from their own account, which replaces the first setup.

Pro

  • Check for conflicts: before booking, the calendar and requests waiting for approval are checked for overlaps. Free (available) events and all-day events don't block timed requests.
  • Require approval: requests wait in Extensions, Form to Calendar Bookings, Requests. Approve adds the event and emails the person; Decline emails your reason.
  • Bookable time slots: the chosen multiple-choice or dropdown question is filled with open times. Taken times disappear after each response and when the sidebar is opened. A meeting you add straight into Calendar is still caught when someone submits that time.
  • Route to calendars by answer: the first matching rule picks the calendar and color.
  • Update the event when a response is edited: needs Allow response editing in the form's settings.
  • Google Meet links, custom reminders, confirmation and decline emails, unlimited forms.

Good to know

  • Events are created on a best-effort basis. Check the Activity log for anything important.
  • Answers are read straight from the form. No spreadsheet is needed and nothing leaves your Google account.
  • Renaming a question is fine. Deleting a mapped question pauses it until you pick another in Edit setup.
