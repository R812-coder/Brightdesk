Turn Pro on in three steps

  1. Find your licence key. It comes by email from BrightDesk with the subject "Your Form to Calendar Bookings licence key", within 24 hours and usually much sooner. Check spam or Promotions if you don't see it.
  2. Paste it in the add-on. Open your form, then Extensions, Form to Calendar Bookings, Open. Choose Upgrade, paste the key under Licence key and click Enter licence key.
  3. Switch on what you need. Choose Edit setup, go to Step 3 and turn on conflict checks, approvals, time slots, routing or Meet links.
The key works for the Google account you paid with. It unlocks Pro when you're signed in to Google with the email address you used at checkout. If you use a different Google account for your forms, tell us both addresses and we'll reissue the key at no cost.

Sharing with your team

Your Organization key works for every Google account on your domain. Send it to your staff: each person pastes the same key in their own sidebar. A Google Workspace admin can also install the add-on for everyone from the Admin console (Apps, Google Workspace Marketplace apps).
