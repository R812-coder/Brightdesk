Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 15, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Changelog for Google Sheets™ ("the Add-on"), developed by BrightDesk ("we," "us," "our"), handles your data.

What data the Add-on accesses

The Add-on accesses the following data within the Google Sheets™ spreadsheet where it is installed:

  • Cell values that are edited (old value and new value)
  • The email address of the user who made the edit (via Google's Session service)
  • Sheet names and cell references of edited cells
  • Timestamps of edits

Where your data is stored

All changelog data is stored entirely within your own Google Sheets™ file, in a dedicated tab called "📋 Changelog." No data is transmitted to BrightDesk servers or any external service. We have zero access to your spreadsheet data.

What data we collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of your spreadsheet data. The only information we may receive is:

  • Your email address — only if you purchase a Pro license (via Stripe) or contact our support
  • Payment information — processed entirely by Stripe; we never see your credit card details

Third-party services

The Add-on itself does not communicate with any third-party services. If you purchase a Pro license, payment is processed by Stripe, which has its own privacy policy.

Permissions requested

The Add-on requests the following Google OAuth scopes:

  • spreadsheets.currentonly — to read cell edits and write changelog entries to the current spreadsheet only
  • script.container.ui — to display the sidebar, settings panel, and dialogs
  • script.send_mail — to send a one-time welcome email when you install the Add-on
  • script.scriptapp — to create the edit-tracking trigger that captures changes

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties. Your data stays in your Google Sheet.

Data retention

Changelog entries persist in your spreadsheet until you choose to delete them. You can clear all history at any time via the Add-on's Settings panel. Uninstalling the Add-on does not delete the Changelog tab — your data remains in your spreadsheet under your control.

Children's privacy

The Add-on is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

AI and machine learning

We do not use your data to develop, improve, or train generalized AI and/or ML models.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at support@brightdesk.dev.
