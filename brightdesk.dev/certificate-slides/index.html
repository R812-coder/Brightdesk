FAQ

Common Questions


Do I need a Google Form?

No. Certificate Slides works with any Google Sheet — just add names, dates, and any other data you want on the certificates. No quiz, no form, no pass threshold required. If you want Forms integration, the Professional plan adds auto-generation on form submission.

How do placeholders work?

Add {{Name}}, {{Date}}, {{Course}}, or any {{ColumnHeader}} text to your Slides template. When you connect a Google Sheet, columns automatically map to matching placeholders. No manual setup needed.

Can I use my own certificate design?

Absolutely. Use any of the 50+ included templates, or design your own certificate from scratch in Google Slides. Add your school logo, brand colors, custom fonts — the add-on works with any Slides design.

Is my data safe?

Yes. All data stays within your Google account. Certificate Slides reads your Sheet data and Slides template, generates PDFs in your Drive, and sends emails from your own Gmail. No data is stored on external servers.

How many certificates can I generate?

Free: 10 per batch. Standard: 200 per batch. Professional: unlimited (auto-continuation for large batches). The batch limit resets each time you run a generation.

What's the "Generated with Certificate Slides" text?

Free-tier certificates include a small attribution footer. This is removed on Standard and Professional plans.
