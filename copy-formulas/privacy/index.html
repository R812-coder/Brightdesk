Privacy Policy

Copy Formulas for Google Sheets — Last updated: March 30, 2026

What Copy Formulas Does

Copy Formulas for Google Sheets is a Google Workspace editor add-on that automatically copies formulas from a template row into new rows created by Google Form submissions. It operates entirely within your Google Sheets spreadsheet.

Data We Access

Copy Formulas accesses only the spreadsheet it is installed on. Specifically, it reads formulas in your designated template row and copies them into new rows. It does not access any other spreadsheets, Google Drive files, emails, contacts, calendars, or any other Google service.

Data We Store

Copy Formulas stores configuration settings (which columns to copy, your template row preference, and an activity log of recent copy operations) using Google Apps Script's DocumentProperties service. This data is stored within your Google account and is tied to the specific spreadsheet where the add-on is installed. We do not store any of your spreadsheet data on external servers.

If you activate a Pro license, your license key and associated email address are stored in DocumentProperties for validation purposes.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of the following: your spreadsheet content or form responses, personal information beyond what is required for license validation, usage analytics or tracking data, cookies or browser fingerprints.

Third-Party Services

Copy Formulas does not connect to any external servers or third-party services. All processing occurs within Google's Apps Script environment. Payment processing for Pro licenses is handled by Stripe. BrightDesk does not store your payment card information — this is managed entirely by Stripe under their own privacy policy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties. Your spreadsheet data never leaves Google's infrastructure.

Data Retention and Deletion

Configuration data is stored as long as the add-on is installed on your spreadsheet. To delete all stored data, uninstall the add-on from your spreadsheet via Extensions > Add-ons > Manage add-ons. This removes all DocumentProperties associated with Copy Formulas.

Children's Privacy

Copy Formulas is a productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
