The add-ons

Three are on the Google Workspace Marketplace now, and Schedule to Calendar Sync is with Google for review.

  • Form to Calendar Bookings New

    Google Forms → Google Calendar

    Each form response becomes a calendar event at the right time, in the right calendar, with the right people invited. Conflict checks, approvals and bookable time slots for rooms, time off and appointments.

    Free plan for one form. Pro and Organization plans for more.

    See how it works

  • Timesheet for Google Calendar Live

    Google Calendar → Google Sheets

    Turns your calendar events into a billable timesheet: hours by project, rates and totals, sorted by event colour or keywords. A Review tab flags overlapping, untitled and uncategorized events before you send an invoice.

    7-day free trial, then Pro.

    Install See how it works

  • Schedule to Calendar Sync In Google's review

    Google Sheets → Google Calendar

    Each row in your sheet becomes a calendar event and stays in step with the row. Edit the row and the same event updates, with no duplicates and times in the right time zone.

    Free plan. Pro and Organization plans for auto-sync and teams.

    See how it works

    You can install it once Google approves it.

  • Notifications for Google Sheets Live

    Google Sheets → Slack, Discord and Google Chat

    Sends a message to your team when a row is added, a cell changes or a value crosses a line you set. No Zapier account needed.

    Free plan. Pro for more rules.

How they're built

They run inside your Google account.
Every add-on is written in Google Apps Script. Your sheets, forms and calendars stay in Google, and there's no BrightDesk server keeping a copy.
They ask for as little access as they can.
Timesheet reads your calendars and never changes them. Schedule to Calendar Sync asks for the spreadsheet you're in and your events, never your other files.
Support comes from the person who built them.
Write in through the support page and you'll usually hear back within a day.
