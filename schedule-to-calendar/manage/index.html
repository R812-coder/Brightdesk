Billing

Update your card, download invoices, switch between monthly and yearly, or cancel. Sign in with the email you paid with; Stripe emails you a one-time code.

Open billing portal

Resend my key

Enter the email you used at checkout. We'll send every active key for it.

Use a different Google account

Keys are tied to one Google account (Pro) or one Google Workspace domain (Organization). Write to us at brightdesk.dev/support with your order reference and the new account, and we'll send a new key within one working day, at no cost.

Monthly plan

Monthly keys last until your next payment plus 7 days. Each renewal emails a fresh key; paste it the same way (Upgrade, "Already have a key?", Activate). The sidebar shows a renewal notice in the last 7 days before a key ends.

When a key ends

Nothing is deleted. Auto-sync stops and its owner gets one email; Pro columns stop applying; Sync now keeps working on the free plan. Paste a new key and Pro is back straight away.

Organization plan: give it to your team

Share the one key with your team. Anyone signed in with an account on your domain can paste it in their own sidebar. A Google Workspace admin can install the add-on for everyone from the Admin console (Apps, Google Workspace Marketplace apps).

Cancelling and refunds

Cancel any time in the billing portal. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then the add-on returns to the free plan. If it didn't work for you, contact us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund, no questions. Full terms are on the terms page.

Anything else: brightdesk.dev/support. We usually reply within 24 hours.
