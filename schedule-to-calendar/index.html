How it works

Set it up once in about a minute. Nothing changes in your calendar until you preview and confirm.

  1. Open the add-on. In your spreadsheet: Extensions, Schedule to Calendar Sync, Open.
  2. Match your columns. Title, date, start, end, location and more, in any order. Most are matched for you.
  3. Pick a calendar. Any calendar you can add events to. If its time zone differs from the sheet's, you choose which one your times mean.
  4. Preview, then sync. See every event it will create, update or remove. A Sync status column then says Synced, or exactly what to fix.

Questions

Does it change events I already have?

No. It creates its own events and never changes or removes events it didn't create, including ones you imported from a sheet before.

What if I sort the sheet?

Sort the whole sheet (Data, Sort sheet) and each row keeps its event. Copying a synced row is fine too: the copy gets its own event.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Everyone who can edit the spreadsheet sees the same setup. Auto-sync runs for the person who turns it on, and the sidebar says who that is.

Do formulas work?

Yes. A date like =A2+7 is read by its result, the same as a typed date. Edits made by formulas sync when the spreadsheet opens (Pro) or when you press Sync now.

What does it store?

Your setup and a short sync history inside the spreadsheet's add-on storage, and three columns in your sheet. Nothing is sent to BrightDesk servers.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The add-on runs inside Google. BrightDesk never receives your sheet or calendar data. Read the privacy policy.
