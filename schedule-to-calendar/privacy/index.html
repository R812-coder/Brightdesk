Schedule to Calendar Sync for Google Sheets™ · Effective 6 November 2026

In short: the add-on runs inside your own Google account. It reads the spreadsheet you open it in and creates events in the calendars you choose. BrightDesk runs no servers for it and never receives your spreadsheet or calendar data.

Who we are

Schedule to Calendar Sync ("the add-on") is made by BrightDesk (brightdesk.dev). Contact us at brightdesk.dev/support.

What the add-on accesses, and why

When you install the add-on, Google asks you to allow the permissions below. The add-on uses each one only for the purpose shown.

PermissionWhat it is used for
View and manage spreadsheets that this application has been installed in
spreadsheets.currentonly		Reads the header row and schedule rows of the tab you choose, in the spreadsheet you opened the add-on in, so each row can become an event. Adds three columns at the end of that tab (Sync ID, Event link and Sync status) and keeps them up to date. It cannot open any other spreadsheet.
View and edit events on all your calendars
calendar.events		Creates, updates, moves and removes the events that come from your rows, in the calendars you choose. It only works with events it created: it finds them by a hidden tag it adds to each one, and never reads, changes or removes any other event.
See the list of Google calendars you're subscribed to
calendar.calendarlist.readonly		Lists the calendars you can add events to, so you can pick one, and reads each calendar's time zone so times land at the right hour. It never changes calendar settings or sharing.

script.send_mail		Only with auto-sync: emails you, at most once a day, if auto-sync fails or stops. Emails go from your account to your own address only.

The add-on has no permission to call outside services, so it cannot send your data anywhere.

Where your data is stored

  • In your spreadsheet: the Sync ID, Event link and Sync status columns the add-on adds to your tab.
  • In the spreadsheet's add-on storage (Google Apps Script document storage, kept with the spreadsheet): your setup (the tab, the header row, which column holds which field, the calendar you picked and its name, your settings and color rules, and the email address of the person who turned auto-sync on); for each synced row, its Sync ID, the calendar it went to and a fingerprint used to tell whether the row changed (not the row's contents); and a history of the last 100 syncs (time, who ran it, how many events were created, updated or removed, and the Sync IDs needed for Undo).
  • In your Google account's add-on storage: your licence key, the ID of the spreadsheet using your free plan, and small markers so the review prompt and failure emails aren't repeated.
  • On each event it creates: the details from your row, a link back to the spreadsheet, and hidden private properties holding the spreadsheet ID, the tab ID and the row's Sync ID, so the add-on can find and update its own events later. They are not shown in Google Calendar.

What we receive

BrightDesk does not receive, copy or store your spreadsheet contents, your calendar data or your emails. The add-on sends no analytics.

When something goes wrong, the add-on writes a short technical error message (for example "mail failed: Service invoked too many times for one day") to Google Cloud Logging in BrightDesk's Google Cloud project, along with an anonymous "first sync" marker so we can count how many people finish setup. These lines contain no spreadsheet contents, event details or email addresses. Google keeps them for 30 days.

If you buy a plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address, the Google account or domain you enter at checkout, the plan bought and payment status, so we can email your licence key and handle billing questions. We never see your full card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.

If you contact support, we receive what you send us and use it only to answer you.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models. Nobody at BrightDesk can read your Google user data, because it never leaves your Google account.

Schedule to Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see what inside your spreadsheet

Anyone who can edit the spreadsheet can open the add-on and see its setup and sync history, including the email address of whoever ran each sync and of whoever turned auto-sync on. Events are created with the calendar access of the person who syncs; with auto-sync, that is the person who turned it on.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Remove the events: in the add-on, Settings, type REMOVE and choose Remove all events. This deletes every event the spreadsheet created, in every calendar it used, and clears the stored per-row records.
  • Remove the columns: delete the Sync ID, Event link and Sync status columns like any other column.
  • Remove your licence key: in the add-on, Upgrade, Remove the licence from this account.
  • Remove the add-on: in Google Sheets, Extensions, Add-ons, Manage add-ons, then the add-on's menu, Uninstall. You can also remove its access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Turn auto-sync off first so its triggers are removed.
  • Your setup and sync history stay in the spreadsheet's add-on storage until the spreadsheet is deleted, so a reinstall picks up where you left off. Deleting the spreadsheet deletes them.
  • Events you don't remove stay on your calendars like any other event.
  • Purchase records are kept as long as tax and accounting rules require.

Security

Your data is processed and stored by Google's infrastructure under your account. The add-on asks for the narrowest permissions that let it work: it can only open the spreadsheet it is used in, and it works only with events, not calendar settings or sharing.

Children

The add-on is meant for organizations and adults managing schedules. It is not directed to children under 13.
