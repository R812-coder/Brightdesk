1. Who we are

Schedule to Calendar Sync ("the add-on") is a Google Sheets™ add-on made by BrightDesk (brightdesk.dev). Contact us at brightdesk.dev/support.

3. Data use: how the data is used

Google user data is used only to provide the features you use in the add-on:

  • Spreadsheet data: to turn each row into an event, to tell when a row has changed, and to write the Sync ID, Event link and Sync status columns back to the tab.
  • Calendar events: to create, update, move and remove the events that come from your rows, when you sync or (with auto-sync) when the sheet changes. Guest email addresses are used only to add those people to the event, and Google sends them invitations if you leave invitations on.
  • Calendar list: to show the calendars you can add events to, so you can pick one, and to read each calendar's time zone so times land at the right hour.
  • Your email address: to check that a licence key belongs to you, and to show other editors of the spreadsheet who turned auto-sync on.
  • Email: only with auto-sync, to email you, at most once a day, if auto-sync fails or stops.

Google user data is not used for advertising, profiling, marketing, analytics, credit or lending decisions, or any purpose other than these features.

4. Where data is stored

All Google user data stays in Google's systems, under your Google account:

  • In your spreadsheet: the Sync ID, Event link and Sync status columns the add-on adds to your tab.
  • In the spreadsheet's add-on storage (Google Apps Script document storage, kept with the spreadsheet): your setup (the tab, the header row, which column holds which field, the calendar you picked and its name, your settings and color rules, and the email address of the person who turned auto-sync on); for each synced row, its Sync ID, the calendar it went to and a fingerprint used to tell whether the row changed (not the row's contents); and a history of the last 100 syncs (time, who ran it, how many events were created, updated or removed, and the Sync IDs needed for Undo).
  • In your Google account's add-on storage: your licence key, the ID of the spreadsheet using your free plan, and small markers so the review prompt and failure emails aren't repeated.
  • On each event the add-on creates: the details from your row, a link back to the spreadsheet, and hidden private properties holding the spreadsheet ID, the tab ID and the row's Sync ID, so the add-on can find and update its own events later. They are not shown in Google Calendar.

5. Data sharing, transfer and disclosure

  • BrightDesk does not receive Google user data, so it does not share, transfer, sell, rent or disclose it to anyone.
  • The add-on has no permission to connect to services outside Google, so it cannot send Google user data anywhere.
  • Inside Google, data goes only where you direct it: events go to the calendars you choose, and invitations go to the guests you list.
  • Google user data is never transferred to advertisers, data brokers, information resellers or AI/ML providers.
  • We would disclose information only if required by law, and only information we actually hold (see section 9), never Google user data, which we do not hold.

6. Data protection: how the data is secured

We treat everything in your spreadsheet and calendar as sensitive data. These are the mechanisms that protect it:

  • Encryption in transit. Every request between your browser, the add-on and Google Sheets, Google Calendar and Gmail travels over HTTPS (TLS). The add-on's sidebar and dialogs are served only over HTTPS by Google.
  • Encryption at rest. Your spreadsheet, the add-on's storage, your calendar events and licence key are stored only on Google's infrastructure, which encrypts data at rest.
  • No external transmission. The add-on runs entirely in Google Apps Script under your account. It has no permission to make network requests to outside servers, so your data never leaves Google's systems.
  • No developer access. Your sign-in and the access you grant are handled by Google's OAuth system. BrightDesk never receives or stores your password or access tokens, and no one at BrightDesk can read your spreadsheet, your calendar or the add-on's stored data.
  • Least-privilege permissions. The add-on can open only the spreadsheet it is used in (spreadsheets.currentonly, not all your spreadsheets), works with events rather than full calendar control (calendar.events, not calendar), and can only read your calendar list. It works only with events it created, identified by a hidden tag.
  • Data minimization. It stores the least it needs: a fingerprint of each row instead of the row's contents, IDs instead of copies of events, and a history capped at the last 100 syncs.
  • Access control through Google. Who can see the setup and history inside a spreadsheet is controlled by that spreadsheet's Google sharing settings; who can see an event is controlled by that calendar's sharing settings. The add-on never changes either.
  • Tamper-proof licence keys. Keys are digitally signed (HMAC-SHA256) and checked inside the add-on, so they cannot be forged. They contain only the plan, the Google account or domain they belong to, and the expiry date.
  • Secured developer accounts. The Google and Stripe accounts BrightDesk uses to publish the add-on and handle payments are protected with 2-Step Verification, and only BrightDesk can access them. Error logs (see section 9) are visible only to the BrightDesk account that owns the Google Cloud project.
  • Payment security. Payments are processed by Stripe, which is certified to PCI DSS Level 1. BrightDesk never sees or stores full card numbers.
  • Incidents. If we learn of a security incident affecting your information, we will notify affected users without undue delay and take steps to contain it. Report a security concern at brightdesk.dev/support.

8. Limited Use and AI/ML

Schedule to Calendar Sync's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data, including data obtained through Google Workspace APIs, is not used to develop, improve or train generalized or non-personalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, and is not transferred to any third-party AI service. The add-on does not use AI or machine-learning models.

9. Other information we receive

  • Error logs. When something goes wrong, the add-on writes a short technical error message (for example "mail failed: Service invoked too many times for one day") to Google Cloud Logging in BrightDesk's Google Cloud project, along with an anonymous "first sync" marker so we can count how many people finish setup. These lines contain no spreadsheet contents, event details or email addresses, and are kept for 30 days.
  • Purchases. If you buy a plan, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address, the Google account or domain you enter at checkout, the plan bought and payment status, and use them only to email your licence key and handle billing questions. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Support. If you contact us, we receive what you send and use it only to answer you.

The add-on sends no analytics and uses no cookies or tracking. We do not sell any of this information.

10. Who can see what inside your spreadsheet

Anyone who can edit the spreadsheet can open the add-on and see its setup and sync history, including the email address of whoever ran each sync and of whoever turned auto-sync on. Events are created with the calendar access of the person who syncs; with auto-sync, that is the person who turned it on.

11. Children

The add-on is meant for organizations and adults managing schedules. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

12. Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for important changes, say so in the add-on. Questions or requests about your data: brightdesk.dev/support.
