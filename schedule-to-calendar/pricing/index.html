Billing questions

How do I get Pro after paying?

Your licence key arrives by email. Open your spreadsheet, then Extensions, Schedule to Calendar Sync, Open, Upgrade, and paste it under "Already have a key?". The key works for the Google account you enter at checkout.

What happens when a key ends?

Nothing is deleted. Auto-sync stops (you get one email), Pro columns stop applying, and Sync now keeps working on the free plan. Paste a new key and Pro is back straight away.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the manage page. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Contact us within 14 days of your first payment and we refund it in full, no questions. See the terms.

Does the Organization plan work with Gmail addresses?

No. It covers one Google Workspace domain, like acme.com. For a personal Gmail account, choose Pro.

Who handles payment?

Stripe. BrightDesk never sees your full card number.
