You're on Pro. Thank you.

Your licence key is on its way to the Google account you entered at checkout. It arrives within 12 hours, usually much sooner.

When it arrives

  1. Open your spreadsheet. Any spreadsheet where you use the add-on.
  2. Extensions, Schedule to Calendar Sync, Open. The sidebar opens on the right.
  3. Upgrade. It's at the bottom of the sidebar's Home screen ("Free plan. Upgrade").
  4. Paste the key under "Already have a key?" and press Activate. You'll see "Pro is active until" and the date.
Didn't get it? Check spam and Promotions, then use Resend my key on the manage page. The key works for the Google account you entered at checkout; if you use a different one, the manage page says how to move it.

Sharing with your team

Your Organization key works for every Google account on your domain. Send it to your team: each person pastes the same key in their own sidebar. A Google Workspace admin can also install the add-on for everyone from the Admin console (Apps, Google Workspace Marketplace apps).

Next: turn on auto-sync

On the sidebar's Home screen, switch on Sync automatically when the sheet changes. Edits then update their events within seconds, and "Also sync when the spreadsheet opens" catches changes made by formulas.
