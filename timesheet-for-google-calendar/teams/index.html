Timesheet for your whole team

One Organization key covers everyone at your Google Workspace domain, up to 50 people, for $499 a year.

How it works

  1. Buy Organization and enter your company domain at checkout, for example yourcompany.com.
  2. Paste the key once. Anyone at your domain opens a Google Sheet and goes to Extensions → Timesheet for Google Calendar → Plans and licence key, then pastes the key.
  3. Everyone is covered. Each person with an @yourcompany.com Google account gets the full add-on the next time they open it, with no key of their own.
  4. Share your setup. The person who activated the key can share their colour mappings, keyword rules and rates as team defaults, so everyone bills to the same projects.

Install it for everyone

Optional, but it saves everyone a step. A Google Workspace admin can install the add-on for the whole domain, or for chosen teams:

Admin console → Apps → Google Workspace Marketplace apps → Apps list → Install app → search for “Timesheet for Google Calendar” → Admin install → choose everyone, or organizational units and groups.

Google’s own instructions: Install Marketplace apps for your organization. Without an admin install, people can still install it themselves from the Marketplace.

Questions

Can we try it first?
Yes. Ask for a free 30-day Organization trial key and tell us your domain. Everyone also gets the normal 7-day trial on their own.
Does it cover personal Gmail accounts?
No. An Organization key covers one company domain. People using personal Gmail accounts need their own Pro plan.
We have more than 50 people.
Email us with your headcount for a quote.
Who can change the team defaults?
The person who activated the key. If they leave, anyone with the key can paste it again to take over.
What does the add-on store about our organization?
The domain, the licence key, who activated it and when, and a one-way code per person to count active users, plus any team defaults you share. Never anything from calendars or spreadsheets. Details are in section 5 of the privacy policy.
