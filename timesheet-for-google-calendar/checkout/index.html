Questions

How do I receive my licence key?
By email, usually within a few hours of paying. Paste it in the add-on under Plans and licence key. The add-on shows your plan and the day it runs until.
Which Google account does a Pro key work for?
The one you enter at checkout as the Google account that will use the add-on. If you paste it while signed in to a different account, the add-on tells you which account the key belongs to. It works in every spreadsheet in that account.
How does the Organization plan work?
You enter your company domain at checkout and get one key. Paste it once in any account at that domain, and everyone with an @yourcompany.com Google account gets the full add-on the next time they open it. Your admin can install the add-on for everyone from the Google Admin console; here’s how. Personal Gmail addresses can’t use an Organization key.
What happens when my subscription renews?
Each yearly renewal brings a new key by email, and each monthly payment brings next month’s key. Ten days before a key ends, the add-on reminds you. Paste the new key and you’re set.
What if I’m not satisfied?
Full refund within 14 days, no questions asked. Email support-calendartimesheet@googlegroups.com.
How do I cancel?
Anytime, from the subscription management page or by emailing support. Your key keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.
