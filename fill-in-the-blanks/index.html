Questions

How do I mark the words to blank out?

Highlight whole words in your document with the highlighter tool in the Google Docs™ toolbar — any color. Then open the add-on sidebar and click Scan document. You can also switch to Word list mode and blank every occurrence of a word you type.

Does it work on long documents and tables?

Yes. The add-on scans the entire document, including multiple pages, tables, and bulleted or numbered lists, and blanks every highlighted word it finds.

Is my document private?

Yes. The add-on only reads and edits the document you have open, and only when you click a button. It never sends your document anywhere. See our privacy policy.

What happens after the free trial?

You keep a free plan that creates worksheets with up to 5 blanks and a word bank. Upgrade to Pro any time for unlimited blanks, hints, numbered blanks, uniform lengths, and answer keys.

Can I get a refund?
