What happens next

Your Pro license key will arrive by email within a few minutes, sent to the address you used at checkout. It looks like FBSK-….

To activate it:

  • Open any Google Doc and go to Extensions → Fill in the Blanks → Open sidebar.
  • Click License key at the bottom of the sidebar.
  • Paste your key and click Activate. The plan badge will switch to Pro.

Didn't get your key within 15 minutes? Check your spam folder, then email support-fillintheblanks@googlegroups.com and we'll sort it out quickly.
