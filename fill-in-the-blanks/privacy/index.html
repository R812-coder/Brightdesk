Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Fill in the Blanks for Google Docs™ ("the add-on") is built by BrightDesk. This policy explains what the add-on accesses and how your information is handled. In short: the add-on works on the document you have open, and does not collect or store the contents of your documents.

What the add-on accesses

  • The current document. When you click a button, the add-on reads the text and highlight formatting of the document you have open, and edits that same document to insert blanks, a word bank, or an answer key. It uses the documents.currentonly permission, which limits access to the single document you are working in. It cannot see your other documents or your Drive.
  • Your email address. Used once to send a one-time welcome email with getting-started instructions, and to match your Pro license. It is not used for marketing lists.

What we store

The add-on saves a small amount of settings data (such as whether your trial has started, whether you dismissed the getting-started card, and your Pro license details) using Google's per-user properties storage tied to your account. The contents of your documents are never sent to us or stored on our servers.

What we do not do

  • We do not transmit, copy, or store the text of your documents.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your data to train any model.

Payments

Pro subscriptions are processed by Stripe. Payment details are handled entirely by Stripe under its own privacy policy; BrightDesk never receives your card information.

Data requests

To ask what is stored or to request deletion, email support-fillintheblanks@googlegroups.com. Uninstalling the add-on removes its stored settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support-fillintheblanks@googlegroups.com.
